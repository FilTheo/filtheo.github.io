Cloud for AI

The Cloud for AI course provides a comprehensive guide to the Machine Learning Operations (MLOps) lifecycle, focusing on the practical challenges of building, deploying, and maintaining reliable ML systems in a production environment. It covers the entire process, from initial project scoping and data strategy to modeling, deployment, and ongoing monitoring.

The curriculum emphasizes the engineering mindset required for robust systems, exploring core concepts such as data leakage, concept drift, online versus batch predictions, and cloud versus edge serving strategies. A key focus is placed on establishing strong baseline models, selecting appropriate business and serving metrics, and starting simple before incrementally increasing model complexity.

The course is hands-on, culminating in a final assignment where students must deliver an end-to-end product. This practical work is supplemented by open-ended questionnaires designed to foster the critical thinking needed to navigate the real-world trade-offs inherent in ML system design.
